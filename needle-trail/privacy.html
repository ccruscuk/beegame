Privacy Policy for Needle Trail

Effective date: 27 November 2026

This policy applies from version 1.1 of the game. Version 1.0 has no ads and no in-app purchases; for version 1.0 only the analytics parts below apply.

Needle Trail ("the game") is a puzzle game for iPhone and Android made by BeeGame ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game collects, why, who receives it, and what you can do about it.

Summary

  • The game is free to play. It shows optional reward ads and short ads between levels, and it sells optional in-app purchases.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. We ask Google for non-personalized ads. The game does not ask for your advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not show the iOS "Allow tracking" prompt.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment card or Apple account details.
  • The game sends gameplay data tied to a random install code, not to your name, to an analytics service so we can find bugs and balance the levels. In some regions the game asks for your permission first (see "Your choices").
  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. There is no account.

What we collect

  • A random install code that the game creates on your device the first time you open it. It is not linked to your name. The first six characters are shown in the game's info panel.
  • Gameplay events: for example when you start, finish or leave a level, the level number, how long it took, how many pieces of thread you undid, how many times you reset a level, and whether and how you used a hint (stock, reward ad or yarn).
  • Ad events: that an ad was offered, started, finished or failed to load, which placement it was (for example between levels or for a hint), and the estimated revenue of the ad.
  • Store events: that you opened a store offer or started or completed a purchase, which product, and the price and currency shown to you.
  • Basic technical data: app version and build, device type and operating system version, your IP address and the approximate country derived from it, and a device identifier that is the same for all apps from the same developer on your device (on iPhone, the identifier for vendors).
  • On your device only: your progress, hints, yarn balance and purchase flags (for example "ads removed"). These are saved on your iPhone and are not sent to us as a profile.

We do not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Why we collect it

To run the game, to understand how many people play, where levels are too hard or confusing, how hints, ads and the Yarn Shop are used, and to fix problems. We do not sell this data and we do not combine it with data from other companies.

Who receives it

  • Unity Technologies processes our analytics data (Unity Analytics) as our service provider ("processor") under its data processing terms. Unity's servers are mainly located in the European Union, with a replicated copy in the United States, so your data may be transferred outside your country. We receive this data from Unity only as reports and statistics.
  • Unity In-App Purchasing completes and verifies your purchases. When you buy, it receives purchase and transaction information (such as the product and price), device and app identifiers, device and technical information, and the approximate country derived from your IP address. Unity states that it uses this data to run the purchase service, to avoid duplicate transactions and to provide revenue reports. This purchase data is sent whether or not you have allowed analytics, because it is needed for the purchase to work.
  • Google (AdMob) provides the ads. Google says the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect: crash logs; performance data such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use; a device identifier (an advertising identifier, or an identifier limited to the app or the developer); information about the ads you have seen; approximate location, including location derived from your IP address; and other interactions such as app launch taps and video views. Google may use this data to show ads, to measure how ads perform and for analytics, including sharing with other parties that show ads. Google handles this data under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites . We cannot see or delete the data that Google collects through its SDK.
  • Apple processes your payment and keeps your purchase records under its own terms. Refund requests go to Apple.

We do not share your data with anyone else.

About ads

  • Ad types: optional reward ads (for a hint, to double a level reward or for free yarn) and ads between levels, which start after level 6. There are no banner ads. A one-time purchase (Remove Ads, or the Ad-Free Bundle) removes the ads between levels; optional reward ads stay.
  • We ask for non-personalized ads in every region. Non-personalized ads are not based on your earlier activity in other apps, but Google may still use limited data such as your IP address, your device and the app's content to deliver and measure ads.
  • We do not ask for your advertising identifier and the game does not show the "Allow tracking" prompt, so iOS does not give Google the advertising identifier.
  • If you tap an ad, it may open a web page or the App Store. That page belongs to the advertiser.

How long we keep it

Unity keeps raw analytics event data for 13 months and then deletes it automatically. Some summary statistics linked to your install code may be kept until they are deleted on request. How long Google, Apple and Unity (for purchases) keep the data they receive is set by their own policies.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Ads consent in Europe: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a form from Google asks for your choices about ads data when you first open the game. You can change your choice at any time in the game's info panel ("Ad privacy options"). Until you choose where this is required, the game does not request ads.
  • Analytics: where permission is required (the region setting of your device is in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or Turkey, or the game cannot tell), the game also asks on first launch whether you agree to gameplay analytics. Until you choose, no analytics data is sent from your device. If you choose "No thanks", you can play the whole game and no analytics data is sent. Elsewhere, analytics is on by default. In every region you can turn analytics on or off at any time in the info panel ("Analytics: On / Off"). When you turn it off, the game stops sending data and sends a request to delete the data already linked to your install code.
  • Ads data: you can limit ad personalization on your device in iOS settings. The game does not ask for tracking permission and ads are non-personalized anyway.
  • Purchases: you can restore earlier purchases with "Restore Purchases" in the Yarn Shop. Manage or ask for refunds through Apple.
  • Other requests: you can ask us to delete or tell you the data linked to your install code. Send an email to info@beemap.io and include the code shown in the game's info panel. For purchase data held by Unity we pass your request on to Unity. Requests about data held by Google or Apple go to those companies. Deleting the game from your device stops further data collection; it does not by itself delete data that was already sent, so please email us if you want it removed.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and is not in the Kids category. It contains ads and in-app purchases. We do not ask for or knowingly collect personal information such as names or email addresses from anyone. The data described above is collected in the same way from every player whose analytics is on. If you are a parent or guardian and want data linked to a code removed, email us.

Internet connection

The game can be played without an internet connection. Ads, purchases and analytics need a connection: when one is available and the matching permission is given, the data described above is sent to Unity and Google. The game opens this privacy policy in your web browser.

Changes

If we change what the game collects, we will update this policy before the new version is released and change the effective date above.
