Needle Trail

Live on the App Storesince Nov 2026

Draw one unbroken thread through every square of a grid, stopping at the numbers in order, and watch the grid become a small stitched pattern.

one thread

How it plays

  1. Drag one thread through every open square exactly once.
  2. Visit the numbered stops in order; drag back to rewind.
  3. No timer, lives or move limit.
