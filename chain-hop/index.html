Chain Hop

Live on the App Storesince 22 Nov 2026

Tap or swipe a log and its front frog hops to the next log, chain the hops and gather one color per log to clear it, in a calm waterfall puzzle.

chain hops

How it plays

  1. Tap a log: its front frog hops to the log below; a full log chains the hop.
  2. Swipe a log up or down to choose the direction.
  3. A log holding one color clears.
