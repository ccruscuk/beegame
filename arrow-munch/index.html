Arrow Munch

Live on the App Storesince 21 Nov 2026

Free the arrows, feed the hungry critters, pop three of a color: a calm iPhone puzzle with no ads, purchases or timers.

tap arrows

How it plays

  1. Tap an arrow with a clear path: it slides off the board onto a shelf.
  2. Three of a color pop and make room.
  3. Critters on the board eat arrows of their own color and open the way.
hearts start at level 3
